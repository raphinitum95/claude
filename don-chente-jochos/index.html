Jocho 101

What's a jocho?

In northern Mexico, a hot dog is a jocho. They're a street-cart classic: grilled, wrapped in bacon, and loaded way past what fits in the bun. Don Chente brought that style to San Antonio's West Side.

  1. Wrap itSausage wrapped in bacon and grilled.
  2. Load itGrilled onion, cheese, asada or pastor.
  3. Finish itChile toreado, salsa, lime. Messy on purpose.

Questions

What is a jocho?

In northern Mexico a hot dog is called a jocho. Ours are wrapped in bacon, grilled, and loaded with grilled onion, cheese, and toppings like asada, al pastor or queso flameado.

Where is Jochos Don Chente?

We're at 12014 Potranco Rd, San Antonio, TX 78253, on the city's far West Side. Get directions.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Order delivery through DoorDash, Uber Eats or Postmates, or call (210) 461-2955 to order ahead for pickup.

Do you cater events?

Yes. We cater birthdays, quinceañeras, weddings, office lunches and more. Send a catering request or call (210) 461-2955.

What are your hours?

Tuesday through Friday, 2:00pm to 9:30pm. Closed Saturday through Monday.
